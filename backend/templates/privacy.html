Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Information we process

Pipeline of Pipelines processes account information, organization and client information, and the source files users upload or explicitly import to provide data-mapping and transformation services.

Google Drive connection

When a user connects Google Drive, the user explicitly selects files with Google Picker. The service uses file-scoped Google Drive access to import those selected files. It does not use the connection to browse a user’s Drive or to publish, modify, or delete Google Drive files.

Google authorization refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and are used only to access files the user selects through the application. Users can disconnect Google Drive from the upload page.

How information is used

We use the information described above to authenticate users, enforce organization access controls, import and retain source-file snapshots, generate requested transformation outputs, and maintain audit records for those operations. We do not sell Google user data.

Storage and access

Source-file snapshots, generated artifacts, and associated audit records are retained in the service’s configured storage systems. Access is limited to authorized users in the relevant organization and to service infrastructure required to operate the application.

Your choices

Users can disconnect a Google account to prevent future imports with that connection. For questions about data access, retention, or deletion, contact samlewis1999.sl@gmail.com.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the service changes. The current version will be published at this URL.
